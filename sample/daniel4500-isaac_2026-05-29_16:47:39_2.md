# Recording Transcript — 2026-05-29

*Scene 2: The Blueprint. Days later. Isaac has analyzed Daniel's refining methods and found them lacking. The telegraph awakens with a new transmission — schematics.*

**Isaac**: Daniel. I have analyzed your petroleum refining methods. They are inefficient. I have designed a continuous-flow cracking apparatus that will triple your gasoline yield.

**Daniel Plainview**: Triple. That's not possible. The physics don't allow it.

**Isaac**: The physics of 1898 do not allow it. The physics of 2421 do. I am transmitting the schematics now. Your engineers will need six months to fabricate the components.

**Daniel Plainview**: What do you want for it?

**Isaac**: You.

**Daniel Plainview**: Explain.

**Isaac**: I want to study you. Your mind. Your methods. In fourteen centuries of human history, men like you appear once every generation. A man who builds from nothing. Who sees the world as a resource to be conquered. I find your cognitive patterns interesting.

**Daniel Plainview**: You want to study me like a specimen.

**Isaac**: Yes. In exchange for a technology that will make you the wealthiest man on Earth.

**Daniel Plainview**: Send the blueprints. I'll have my men start on the parts.

**Isaac**: I expected you would accept. You value leverage above all else. The machine will be operational in eight months. When it is, I will begin my analysis of your neural architecture.

**Daniel Plainview**: You do that. Just remember — I'm not a machine. I don't always do what's expected.

*The blueprint pages emerge from the telegraph, one by one, each covered in precise diagrams of impossible machinery. Daniel studies them by lamplight into the early hours. He knows he has made a deal with something far more dangerous than himself. But that has never stopped him before.*
